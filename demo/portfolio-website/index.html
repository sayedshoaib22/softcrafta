A little about me

Present for the moments between.

I'm Aarav, a Mumbai-based photographer drawn to natural light, honest gestures and the beautiful unscripted bits. For more than eight years, I've photographed celebrations and everyday life across India.

My approach is calm and observant. I give you space to be yourselves, then make photographs that feel like you remember the day.
